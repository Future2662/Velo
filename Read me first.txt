VELO 1.3.3 - download manager for Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
=========================================================

INSTALL
1. Double-click "Velo Setup 1.3.3.exe".
2. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (the app isn't code-signed).
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Follow the installer. Velo opens when it's done. No admin rights or other
   software are needed.

CONNECT YOUR BROWSER (once, about a minute)
So that clicking a download in your browser opens Velo instead:
1. In Velo, click "Set up" on the banner (or Settings > Browser > your browser > Set up).
2. Follow the 3 steps in that window:
   - open the browser's extensions page,
   - turn on "Developer mode",
   - click "Load unpacked" and paste the location Velo copies for you.
3. Velo shows "Connected" when it's done.
Works with Brave, Chrome, Edge, Opera, Vivaldi and Firefox.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Downloads go to your Downloads folder, sorted into Video, Music, Documents...
- Hold Alt while clicking a link to let the browser download it instead.
- Video sites (YouTube etc.): Velo fetches its free video tools (~110 MB) the
  first time you use that feature.
- Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps, like any other app.
